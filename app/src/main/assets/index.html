Edappadi Kadai • com.edappadikadai.app

Edappadi Kadai Privacy Policy

Effective: August 2026 | Meat & Vegetable Delivery in Tamil Nadu, India

1. 📋 Data We Collect

Name, phone number, email (optional), delivery address, GPS location coordinates (lat/lng), active cart items, complete order history, and UPI transaction reference ID / approval ref no.

🔒 Zero Bank Details: We NEVER collect or store Bank Accounts, Debit/Credit Card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs.

2. ⚙️ How Data is Used

  • Account authentication (Firebase Phone Auth & Google Sign-In)
  • Order preparation and doorstep delivery routing
  • Real-time push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
  • Customer support & loyalty rewards point calculation

3. ☁️ Google Firebase Infrastructure

We use Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (database), Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging (FCM), Cloud Functions, Crashlytics, and App Check (Play Integrity).

4. 💳 UPI Payments (Intent Flow)

Payment occurs inside your installed UPI app (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm/BHIM via upi:// deep link). Our app only receives the transaction reference number and status.

5. 🔒 Security & Encryption

100% HTTPS network traffic (cleartext blocked), AES-256 GCM encrypted local storage via Android Keystore, and SHA-256 hashed credentials.

6. 🚫 Third-Party Data Sharing

Data is NOT shared with third parties or advertisers. Zero third-party ad networks or data broker trackers.

7. 🗑️ Data Retention & Deletion

Retained while account is active. Users can delete accounts anytime via the in-app Delete Account option or by contacting admin (via deleteCustomerAccount Cloud Function).

8. 👶 Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for or directed to children under 18 years of age.

  • வாடிக்கையாளர் உதவி மற்றும் ரிவார்ட் பாயிண்ட் மேலாண்மை
